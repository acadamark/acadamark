# `<book-body>`

The body region of a `<book>`. Generated by `acadamarkBookStructuring` to hold the book's main content — its book-parts. Rarely authored directly.

## Contents

The structural plugin places book-parts (chapters and named parts) into `<book-body>`. Specifically, book-parts with `book-part-type` values: `chapter`, `part`, `introduction`, `conclusion`, `other` (default).

Body-level prose (paragraphs, sections) is **not** typically a direct child of `<book-body>` — prose belongs inside book-parts. The schema accepts only `<book-part>` children at this level.

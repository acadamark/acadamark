# `<article-back>`

The back-matter region of an `<article>`. Auto-generated by the structural plugin when authors omit explicit region wrappers; rarely authored directly. The explicit form is available when authors need fine control over which children belong in which region.

Back-matter children: bibliography, appendices, note-list, and other closing block content.

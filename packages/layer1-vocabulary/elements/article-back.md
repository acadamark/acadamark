# `<article-back>`

The back-matter region of an `<article>`. Generated by `enscribeArticleStructuring` to hold apparatus that follows the body content. Rarely authored directly.

## Contents

The structural plugin places these children inside `<article-back>`:

- `<bibliography>` — the rendered bibliography. Auto-generated by `enscribeBibliographyAssembly` from the citation registry when not authored explicitly.
- `<note-list>` — collected notes. Auto-generated by `enscribeNotePlacement` when notes use a collecting `placement` (`end`/`foot`); `note-scope` governs whether they collect here or per chapter/section.
- `<book-part book-part-type="appendix">` — article appendices. Authored as `<appendix | Title>` and lowered by `enscribeArticleStructuring`, which places them here (an article reuses the book-part appendix projection).
- `<data>` blocks (by default; authors can place them in front matter instead).
- `<config>` blocks (by default; same).

# `<book-back>`

The back-matter region of a `<book>`. Generated by `enscribeBookStructuring` to hold apparatus and back-matter book-parts. Rarely authored directly.

## Contents

The structural plugin places these children inside `<book-back>`:

- Book-parts with back-matter `book-part-type` values: `appendix`, `glossary`, `colophon`.
- `<bibliography>` — the rendered bibliography. Auto-generated by `enscribeBibliographyAssembly`.
- `<note-list>` — collected end-notes or chapter-end notes. Auto-generated by `enscribeNotePlacement`.
- `<data>` and `<config>` blocks (by default; authors can place them in front matter instead).
